# Vehicle Models 
  In this document are all the physical versions of the vehicle and a short description of each model.
 
 ## V-1.0 (Feb/21/26)
This design was made using 3D printing. It is a small vehicle that has an ultrasonic sensor to detect when it should turn the front wheels. On top of the robot, the battery is located, and above that is the Maker Pi controller, which processes all the data. This model doesn't detect colors.

## V-1.4 (Apr/29/26)
The new version has several things added. It now has an Adafruit BNO085 IMU above the steering servo, which tracks the car’s heading and turning. It also has a SparkFun APDS-9960 sensor at the front between the two distance sensors, and small distance sensors in gold brackets on the left and right sides to detect walls. The frame is stronger, with full black side rails, cross-members, rings at the front and rear, and a redesigned pink spine that holds the electronics. All four wheels now have rubber off-road tires, and the wiring uses colored jumper wires with a USB-C power module near the controller’s VIN input. What the car already had and kept is its 3D-printed body (black frame, pink spine, gold hubs, brackets and gears), the purple Cytron Motion 2350 Pro controller on top, a black servo that steers the front wheels through a pink rack-and-pinion, two red SparkFun dToF distance sensors (TMF8820/TMF8821) at the front, and a yellow DC gear motor that drives the rear through a gold gear and axle assembly with metal bearings in the hubs.

## V-1.5 (Sept/12/26)
Version 1.5 has a few things added. A power bank (with C1 and C2 USB-C in/out ports and a USB-A out port) sits on top of the car and powers it, so the car no longer needs a cable to the bench supply. The purple Cytron Motion 2350 Pro controller is mounted on top of the power bank and held in place by gold 3D-printed clips. At the front there is a gold V-shaped printed bumper. The front section is simpler, with a black servo and a pink rack-and-pinion, and the cabling is neat, with colored servo cables and small plug-in connectors.
What the car already had and kept is its 3D-printed body, with a black frame (side rails with rings at the front and rear), a pink spine on black posts, and gold hubs, brackets and gears. It still has the rack-and-pinion steering, the yellow DC gear motor driving the rear through a black gear block and a large gold gear, and the metal bearings in the hubs. All four wheels have rubber off-road tires. The side distance sensors (red boards in gold brackets, one on each side) are still there to detect walls.

In these photos I can’t see the BNO085 IMU, the APDS-9960, or the two red dToF sensors at the front. They may have been removed or moved, or they may just be out of view, so check this against your car before you write it in the README.

## V-1.6 (Nov/09/26)
Version 1.6 has two black color sensor boards mounted at an angle on the gold V-shaped printed mount at the front. The small print on one of them appears to read TCS3400. Each board has a color sensor and white LEDs, and they connect to the controller through purple, white, grey and yellow jumper wires. The power bank is the onn one, and it has a USB-A port and two USB-C ports. It sits in a pink 3D-printed cradle with a U-shaped holder, and the purple Cytron Motion 2350 Pro controller sits on top of it. A rubber band holds the power bank and controller down, and another rubber band goes around the steering servo and chassis. A small blue USB-C module beside the controller’s VIN input brings the power bank’s power into the board. The red side sensors are now clearly labeled SparkFun dToF TMF8821. One sits in a gold bracket on each side of the car and measures the distance to the walls.
What the car already had and kept is the 3D-printed body, with a black frame (side rails with rings at the front and rear), a pink spine on black posts, and gold hubs, brackets and gears. The large black servo steers through the pink pinion and rack. The yellow DC gear motor, held by a cable tie, drives the rear through the black gear block and the large gold gear on the rear axle. The gold rear bracket has a ring guide, and the wheels have metal bearings in the hubs. All four wheels have black rubber off-road tires.
